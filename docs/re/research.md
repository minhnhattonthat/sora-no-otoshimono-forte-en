## Game
- "Touch communication" adventure. Story mode + action select; raise 5 stats (title changes), 40+ touch scenes.
- Original story: 7 wish cards go berserk; 6 chapters (+ prologue, Ch7 epilogue script).
  Chapter titles: Ch1 服よさらば, Ch2 智樹先生ありがとう, Ch3 こすれ、トモキ, Ch4 煩悩は爆発だ！, Ch5 妄想はどこまでも, Ch6 ＋１人いる！
- 7 endings: Ikaros, Nymph, Astraea, Sohara, Mikako (President), Sugata, Tomoko. Touch scenes voiced.

## Official English names
- The manga (Suu Minazuki) was never licensed in English. Official English = Funimation anime "Heaven's Lost Property".
- Funimation: Tomoki Sakurai, Ikaros, Nymph, Astraea, Sohara Mitsuki, Eishiro Sugata, Mikako Satsukitane, Chaos,
  Daedalus, Tomoko, Hiyori Kazane. Terms: Angeloid, Synapse, Uranus Queen, Pandora, Apollon, Aegis, Artemis,
  Chrysaor, Sorami City, New World Discovery Club, Downer (地蟲).
