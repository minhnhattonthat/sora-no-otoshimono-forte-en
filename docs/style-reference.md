# Style reference (from the pilot, story/ch00_000 = batch b000)

## Voices
| Character | Voice | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tomoki | casual, perverted, loud retorts; inner narration wry and self-pitying | 「どういうわけだっ！ なんで、アンタは勝手に人ん家でメシ食ってんだよ！？」 -> "And so" what?! Why are you eating in somebody else's house without asking?! |
| Ikaros | minimal, polite, halting, literal | 「おはようございます……マスター。」 -> Good morning... Master. |
| Nymph | sharp, bratty, secretly caring; "Alpha", "Delta", "Downers" | 「……心配して損したじゃない！ もう、顔でも洗ってきなさいよ！」 -> ...And I was actually worried about you! Ugh, just go wash your face already! |
| Astraea | childish whining, food-obsessed, "-senpai" for the other Angeloids | 「ちぇ～、ニンフ先輩のイジワル。」 -> Hmph~ Nymph-senpai, you meanie. |
| Sohara | gentle until angry; "Tomo-chan" | 「ト～モ～ちゃ～～ん？ 朝っぱらからなにやってるのかなぁ？」 -> To~mo~cha~a~n? What do you think you're doing first thing in the morning? |
| Sugata | deadpan, formal, lecturing | 「……状況を整理しよう。」 -> ...Let us assess the situation. |
| Mikako | sweet, teasing, third person "the President" | 「いいじゃないの～。楽しいから。」 -> Oh, what's the harm~? It's fun. |

## Paired examples
- 説明：バカ。以上。 -> Description: Idiot. The end.
- 空は飛ぶわ、常識ないわ、学校来るわ、変なカードでシナプスのアイテム呼び出して騒ぎになるわ……。
  -> She flies, she has zero common sense, she comes to school, she summons Synapse items with weird Cards and causes chaos...
- このおっぱいの大きい女は見月そはらといって、隣に住んでる俺の幼なじみなんだけど……
  -> This girl with the big boobs is Sohara Mitsuki, my childhood friend who lives next door, but...
- どしーん！ -> Thuuud!   どごおっ！！ -> WHAM!!
- Corrected example: 会長、そういうのは、まだ早いと思うわ～。 is Mikako about herself, not addressing the President:
  -> The President thinks that sort of thing is still a bit too soon~
